# Sources of the held-out photos

Every photo here is from **The Metropolitan Museum of Art**'s Open Access collection: images of
public-domain works that the museum releases under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**, free to use
for any purpose without permission ([the museum's image policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources): "available for unrestricted use, and at no cost"). Every object used is marked public domain in the museum's data (`isPublicDomain`).
They were found through the museum's Collection API on 29 October 2026, chosen because the museum
photographed the same object more than once: two of its photographs of one object are an
independently taken pair, which a crop of one photograph is not.

Photos are resized to at most 1,024 pixels on a side (the size of the app's stills) and saved as
JPEG; otherwise unchanged, except the two degraded copies listed last.
